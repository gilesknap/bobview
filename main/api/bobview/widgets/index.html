slider 🔒
Phoebus ScaledSlider and ScrollBar: a groove along the widget with a thumb at the value, and for a scaled slider with show_scale labelled ticks on one side and its alarm level markers on the other, as Phoebus’ JavaFX Slider; no value box. A click or drag moves the thumb, and its value is written when the button is let go, over the range slider_range gives.
slider_layout 🔒
Lay out a slider in rect: the thumb’s band, the scale on its far side (below a horizontal slider, right of a vertical one) and the level markers on its near side, all centred across it, as a JavaFX Slider centres its track and tick marks. scale is the room the scale’s labels take across the slider and half of their length along it, and markers the room the markers’ labels take across it; None leaves either out. What the box has no room for is left out, the markers first, then the scale’s labels and then its ticks, before the thumb gets thin.
slider_markers 🔒
A scaled slider’s alarm level markers, as Phoebus’: each of LOLO, LOW, HIGH and HIHI there is a level for, unless its show_* is false. The levels are the PV’s with limits_from_pv while it is connected, else the widget’s level_* (10, 20, 80 and 90 unless given). Room is kept for them all; those outside the range are not drawn.
slider_range 🔒
The range a slider runs over, from its minimum and maximum or its PV’s: as Phoebus, 0 to 100 for one that is empty or inverted. A BOY slider (the bobview-only <boy_range> from the .opi import) keeps an inverted range, running from the maximum, and widens an empty one to the minimum plus one, as BOY’s scale did.

touch_targets
Grow the hit area of every control in touch mode, without changing how anything is drawn. egui hit-tests in two steps: a touch directly on a control always goes to that control (the topmost one), and only a touch on no control at all goes to the nearest control within interact_radius. So a small control gets a finger-sized target in the space around it, but never takes a touch from a neighbour that was hit directly, and the gap between two controls is shared by distance.
