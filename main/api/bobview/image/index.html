The image widget: a PVA NTNDArray (or a CA waveform shaped by data_width x data_height) drawn through a colour map, with a colour bar and, when show_toolbar is set, the Phoebus zoom/pan/crosshair toolbar.

Identifies what a texture shows: the frame’s unique id, its data and the display settings. The data is held, not just its address: a CA array is freed when the next one arrives, and an allocator may give the next one the same address, which would leave the old picture on screen.
