bobview

Widgets

The Phoebus Display Builder widgets bobview draws, and how far it goes with each. BOY .opi widgets are translated to these; see BOY widgets.

widgetsnotes
label, textupdate, textentryformats: default, decimal, exponential, engineering, hex, compact, string, sexagesimal (also HMS and DMS), binary, significant; units, precision; typed text is parsed as Phoebus does (units dropped, 0x/0b numbers, enum labels)
led, multi_state_led, byte_monitor
action_button, bool_button, choice, radio, combo, checkboxopen display and write PV actions
scaledslider, scrollbar, spinner
linearmeterscale, warning regions from the levels (the PV's alarm limits with limits_from_pv), limit markers, needle; horizontal or vertical
metera dial: scale (linear or log_scale), warning regions from the levels (the PV's alarm limits with limits_from_pv), needle
progressbar, tank, thermometerone shared bar renderer (tank and thermometer are vertical bars with their own default fill colours)
xyplotline, step and bar traces with markers against index or an x PV; several y axes, autoscale, log scale, grid, legend
stripchartits traces' PVs over the last time_range, sampled from when the display opened, on the first y axis (autoscale or fixed, log scale, grid), legend
rectangle, ellipse, arc, polyline, polygondashed and dotted line_style for rectangles and polylines
group, tabs, embedded, navtabsmacros pass down; embedded resize modes 0–2 and 4 (crop)
imagePVA NTNDArray (uncompressed, mono or RGB1) or a CA array shaped by data_width/data_height; Phoebus colour maps, autoscale or fixed range, unsigned, colour bar with a scale; toolbar to zoom, pan and place a crosshair that reads out the pixel value; decimated when a side is over 2048 pixels, to keep drawing fast
symbol, pictureSVG or PNG files, local or fetched next to the display; the PV value picks the symbol (initial_index, array_index, fallback_symbol; an empty one draws nothing), rotation, preserve_ratio/stretch_image, opacity, overlay for a disconnected PV; run_actions_on_mouse_click runs the symbol's actions when clicked; images inside an SVG are drawn only when embedded (data: URLs); a strip of frames sub_image_width wide (DLS EDM symbols) shows frame image_index

Common to all widgets

border_width/border_color borders, alarm borders, dashed borders for disconnected PVs, and label and text update rotation_step. Macros and rules are described in Macros, rules and PV types.

Unsupported

Unsupported widgets, such as table, webbrowser, array, fileselector and byte-array, are drawn as a grey placeholder labelled with the widget type and PV name, and counted in the status line.

Not yet

  • PV Access field paths (pva://name/field), and sys:// and other PV types (shown as not supported)
  • compressed (codec) images, image axes, ROIs and cursor PVs
  • the XY plot markers, error bars and toolbar
  • the strip chart's toolbar and second y axes
  • GIF and JPEG images (symbols and pictures show their fallback or a placeholder), and text inside SVG images
  • scripts: an execute action logs that it is not supported, except the "toggle the widget's PV" script that techui-builder puts in synoptic symbols, which bobview recognises and runs itself
