Module polygon 

Source
Expand description

Filling polygons that may be concave (L shapes, arrows, synoptic outlines), which egui’s convex_polygon fills wrongly.

Structs§

Cache 🔒
The triangles worked out for outlines, in two generations so that it stays bounded when a rule keeps changing a polygon’s points: once the newer is full it becomes the older and the older is dropped. An outline still in use is found in the older and moved to the newer, so it is not cut again.

Constants§

CACHE_SIZE 🔒
How many outlines the cache takes before it starts a new generation.
MAX_POINTS 🔒
Outlines with more points than this are filled as a fan, as before: ear clipping is quadratic or worse in the number of points.

Functions§

area2 🔒
Twice the signed area of pts: positive when they go anticlockwise in y-up coordinates (clockwise on a y-down screen).
cross 🔒
fill
Fill the polygon screen, the points display placed on the screen. A convex one is filled by egui, with anti-aliased edges; a concave one is cut into triangles, worked out once for its display points (moving or zooming it does not change them) and kept in a bounded cache in egui’s memory.
in_triangle 🔒
Whether p is inside or on the triangle a b c, which turns the way sign says.
triangulate
The triangles filling the outline pts, three indices into it each, by ear clipping; None for a convex outline (egui fills those itself) or one too big to cut. An outline that crosses itself runs out of ears at some point, and what is left is filled as a fan.
